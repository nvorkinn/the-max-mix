# How to use your music screen

THE MAX MIX is a touchscreen that plays Spotify. It updates itself, so you only need the screen and a Spotify Premium
account. The words in bold are what you'll see on the screen.

## 1. Get it online

1. Plug it in. With no known Wi-Fi it opens **Choose a Wi-Fi network**.
2. Tap your network, type the password on the on-screen keyboard and tap **Connect**.
3. Your network isn't listed? Tap **Refresh**, or **Other…** to type its name.

## 2. Sign in to Spotify

1. Tap **Connect Spotify**.
2. Sign in on Spotify's page with your email and password and agree. A keyboard appears when you tap a box; **Done**
   hides it. The Google, Apple and Facebook buttons don't get a keyboard, so use your email instead.
3. You only do this once. To switch account, open Settings (the gear, top right), tap **Sign out**, then **Log in with
   Spotify**.

## 3. Pick where it plays

- Tap the speaker name at the bottom left to open the speakers list.
- **This tablet** plays through the screen. Choose it first if you want AirPlay speakers such as Sonos, then tap one or
  more of them so they show **Selected**. A speaker that was loud starts at a low volume; use the main volume slider.
  Each speaker's own volume slider shows only after you tap the sliders button at the top of the list.
- Spotify Connect speakers are listed too. Open Spotify on them once so they show up.

## 4. Choose music

- Tap the magnifying glass at the top to open **Choose music**: Liked Songs, your playlists, what you played recently,
  or **Search** to find any song, album or playlist.
- Tap a song in the queue on the right to jump to it.
- Tap the heart to save a song to Liked Songs.
- Tap the microphone on the cover to show the lyrics, and the picture button to bring the cover back.

## 5. Party with friends

Start a Jam in the Spotify app on your phone while the screen is playing. Guests add songs from their own Spotify app,
and the songs appear in the queue after a moment.

## 6. Make it yours

- Settings, **Colour**: eight colours for the screen.
- Settings, **Screen sleep**: the screen goes dark after 5 minutes without music. Choose 2, 5, 15 or 30 min, or
  **Never**.
- The "zzz" button at the top puts it to sleep straight away. Touch the screen to wake it.
- New Wi-Fi? Settings, **Wi-Fi network**.

## If something's wrong

- **Music stops or a banner shows a problem:** tap the button on the banner. If that doesn't help, Settings, **Restart
  kiosk** (tap twice to confirm).
- **The screen is stuck or blank:** it usually recovers by itself within a few minutes. If not, Settings, **Reboot Pi**,
  or unplug it for ten seconds.
- **Updates:** they install by themselves and the screen restarts at 4:30 in the morning. To get one now, Settings,
  **Update now**. Don't unplug it while an update is running.
- **Spotify says you can't use it:** check that your account is Premium, then tell Nikolai, who may need to add your
  account to the app.

Still stuck? Message Nikolai, who can often check on it from home.
